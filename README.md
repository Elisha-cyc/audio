# Common Ground — POLI 111 audiobook

A two-person, responsive audiobook and study guide. Chapters are original summaries based on the POLI111 Combined Slides, Ruth POLI lecture slides, The ABC of Political Science by Isaac Brako and Kwame Asah-Asante, and Marcus POLI111 past questions. Identical PDF copies are treated as duplicates.

The book covers political science definitions and scope, state/nation, power and sovereignty, ideology, government forms and systems, democracy, governance, participation and exam methods. The first chapter compares Aristotle, Lasswell, Easton and Dahl, with four strengths and limitations for each. Research sources are linked in the app.

Read aloud uses the browser speech voices on the device. Progress is stored in that browser. Two partners can take turns on one device.

The World Today card refreshes global English-language headlines on page load using the GDELT DOC API and links to original publishers, with AP, Reuters and Ghana News Agency fallbacks.

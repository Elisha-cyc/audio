# Common Ground — POLI 111 audiobook

A two-person, responsive audiobook and study guide for University of Ghana POLI 111. The course retains its existing introductory lessons and the six textbook-aligned chapters based on *The ABC of Political Science* by Isaac Brako and Kwame Asah-Asante.

The POLI 111 library now also includes 13 essay-style chapters guided by the chapter and subtopic outline of *Understanding Political Institutions* by Kwame Asah-Asante and Isaac Brako. The chapters cover political institutions and institutional theory; the legislature; executive; judiciary; separation of powers and checks and balances; rule of law; bureaucracy; delegated legislation; political parties; interest groups; electoral processes; electoral systems; and constitutions. Each subtopic is written as a connected explanation, with practical examples from ordinary life and Ghanaian society to help students understand concepts and develop essay answers.

The institutions lessons are original study explanations guided by the book's contents, not reproduced passages from the book. The in-app textbook contents browser lists all 19 textbook chapters and opens each topic directly in the audiobook. The matching course PDF uses the same lesson text.

Read aloud uses the browser speech voices on the device. Progress is stored in that browser. Two partners can take turns on one device.

The World Today card refreshes global English-language headlines on page load using the GDELT DOC API and links to original publishers, with AP, Reuters and Ghana News Agency fallbacks.
